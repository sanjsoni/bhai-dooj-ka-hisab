# 🪔 Bhai Dooj Ka Hisaab (Shagun Calculator)

A festive, desi-meme web app built for **Bhai Dooj** celebrating the eternal brother-sister bond with affectionate roasting, comedy, and shagun calculation!

> **Tagline**: *"Shagun Calculator — Bhai ke wallet ka sach, ab behna ke haath mein!"* 💸🪔

🔗 **Live Link**: [bhaidoojkahisaab.vercel.app](https://bhaidoojkahisaab.vercel.app/)

---

## 💡 Concept & Highlights

- **🌸 Behna Mode ("Main Behna Hoon")**:
  - The sister answers 7 funny questions about her brother's daily antics, stolen chocolates, and delayed gifts.
  - Calculates the official shagun penalty he owes her. The sister always wins!
- **🔥 Bhai Mode ("Main Bhai Hoon")**:
  - The brother confesses his guilt and answers funny questions about himself.
  - Can enter the amount he was *planning* to give, and the app compares his plan with the official hisaab (Pakde gaye / Sahi plan / Maha-daani).
- **💌 Shagun Lifafa & "Behna Ka Shagun" Stamp**:
  - Displays the shagun amount, followed by an Indian festive Shagun Envelope with a ₹1 coin and an authentic rubber stamp of **"Behna Ka Shagun"** (100% Non-Refundable ✅).
- **📸 High-Resolution Instagram Story Card (1080x1920)**:
  - Generates a 1080x1920 Story Card directly in the browser via HTML5 Canvas for instant downloading.
- **📲 WhatsApp Cross-Sharing**:
  - Pre-filled funny roast links to send directly to Bhai or Behna on WhatsApp.

---

## 🛠️ Built With

- **HTML5 & Modern Vanilla JavaScript** (Zero external libraries, zero build steps)
- **Custom CSS3** (Festive marigold, amber & royal crimson palette; mobile-first responsive layout; `prefers-reduced-motion` support)
- **HTML5 Canvas API** (Client-side 1080x1920 story card generation)
